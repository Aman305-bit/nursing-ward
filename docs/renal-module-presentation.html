Renal Module Documentation

Folder-wise code map, screen-wise purpose, role-wise access, and presentation flow for the developed renal module.

Project: Plasmit Hospital HMS
Date: 24 May 2026
Main module route: /renal
Data mode: Static/mock workflow, production-style UI
Short explanation for presentation: Renal module is a role-aware clinical workspace for urine output, intake/output charting, drains/devices, renal labs, renal alerts, dialysis billing, reports, and final doctor sign-off. The module is designed so each user sees what they can do based on their role.

High-level workflow

1. Patient / Chart
Open existing renal chart or create a new renal chart.
2. Clinical Entry
Nurse/doctor records I/O, drains, urine output, and notes.
3. Review
Alerts, labs, orders, balance trends, and dialysis needs are reviewed.
4. Sign / Report
Doctor signs report, then report goes to EMR or billing handoff.
This document is generated for presentation and handover of the developed renal screens.

My Actions tab

Action Allowed role When to use After submit
Add I/O entry Nurse / Doctor During bedside hourly or shift charting. Fluid balance table and renal chart get updated.
Acknowledge alert Nurse / Doctor When a renal rule needs acknowledgement or escalation. Alert action is captured for audit and handoff.
Sign renal report Doctor After I/O, labs, alerts, and orders are reviewed. Report becomes ready for signed clinical handoff.
Add renal order Doctor When follow-up action is required, such as repeat RFT or dialysis review. Order appears in labs, orders, and sign-off checklist.
Update renal labs Lab / Doctor After a renal sample result is available. Latest renal labs and abnormal flags get refreshed.
Prepare dialysis billing Billing When dialysis session status is billing pending. Billing receives service rows without clinical chart edits.

Individual Patient Chart

Tab / Section Use Main actions

Presentation Script

  1. Start from sidebar: Show Renal menu and submenus: Overview, Patient Charts, Fluid Balance, Drains & Devices, Renal Labs, Reports.
  2. Explain role banner: Screen changes based on selected role. Nurse sees charting actions, Doctor sees review/sign-off, Lab sees lab update, Billing sees billing handoff.
  3. Overview: Show Patients, Renal Alert Queue, and My Actions. Explain that My Actions tells each user what they can do now.
  4. New renal entry: Show dropdown-based patient/chart selection. Explain existing chart vs new chart scenario.
  5. Patient detail: Open one patient and show tabs: Overview, I/O Chart, Labs & Orders, Notes & Sign-off.
  6. Drains: Show add drain reading modal and review modal. Explain device/site dropdowns.
  7. Labs: Show search/filter/sort, update lab modal, review lab modal.
  8. Reports: Show report scope: all patients, individual patient, selected patients. Explain EMR handoff and dialysis billing.
  9. End with access: Explain role matrix and why locked buttons protect clinical workflows.

One-minute summary

Renal module is a complete workflow from bedside I/O entry to doctor sign-off and reporting. Nurse enters I/O and drains, Lab updates renal investigations, Doctor reviews alerts/orders and signs report, Billing handles dialysis billing, and Management can review read-only dashboards. Every action opens in a focused centered modal and is designed for quick use in hospital operations.
